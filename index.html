Drag this button onto your bookmarks bar (show it with Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + B).

Usage

  1. Open a PDF in Google Drive and let the first page load.
  2. Click the bookmark. It scrolls through each page, captures it, and downloads the PDF.
  3. Keep the tab open and visible until it finishes.

PDF only. For video, use the extension.
