Drive PDF Export

Bookmarklet version of the Drive Preview Exporter PDF export. Runs in your browser, loads nothing external.

Drive PDF Export
  1. Drag the button onto your bookmarks bar. (Or make a new bookmark and paste the contents of bookmarklet.txt as its URL.)
  2. Open a PDF in Google Drive.
  3. Click the bookmark. It scrolls to each page, captures it, and downloads the PDF. Keep the tab open and visible until it finishes.
For sharper pages, zoom the document to 200% or more first. Use only on files you have the right to save.
